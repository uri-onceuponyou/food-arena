Standing constraints

  • The game design is frozen. Damage, cooldowns, ranges, speeds and match structure are transcribed verbatim from the 2D prototype and must not be retuned.
  • The arena is the one sanctioned exception — layout and props are open, as long as a central hazard, physical cover and a slowing hazard survive.
  • Every piece is judged on rendered pixels in a blind A/B against real Brawl Stars and Zooba stills, never on a description of the work.
  • Art direction was corrected mid-flight: the brief said cel-shaded, but the reference plates show smooth-shaded high-key vinyl-toy surfaces with almost no outline. The brief says the bar wins over its own prose.
  • A shared chibi rig now underpins every character: the food is the head and identity mass, the rig supplies limbs, poses and one motion vocabulary so the cast reads as one production.
